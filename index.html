Deepak Singh

Director | Registered Psychologist

Dr. Singh provides therapy for children, adolescents, families, and young adults, as well as psychological assessment for children, adolescents, and adults. He strives to create a warm, welcoming environment where clients feel comfortable, understood, and at home.

Dr. Singh brings extensive clinical experience, specialized training, and a genuine curiosity about each person he works with. He believes effective psychological care begins with trust and connection. His approach is collaborative, flexible, and individualized, focusing on each client's strengths, experiences, relationships, and goals.

Dr. Singh has a particular passion for psychological assessment, viewing it as a puzzle in which each piece helps explain how a person thinks, learns, feels, and functions. He assesses a variety of concerns, including but not limited to ADHD, Learning Disabilities, Autism Spectrum Disorder, Intellectual Disability, Giftedness, anxiety, depression, OCD, and trauma-related concerns.

In therapy, Dr. Singh uses evidence-based approaches including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Emotion-Focused Family Therapy (EFFT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT), Interpersonal Therapy, and play therapy. He works with anxiety, OCD, depression, ADHD, emotion regulation, life transitions, grief, parent-child relationships, and behavioural difficulties. He believes therapy should be engaging and flexible. He gently challenges clients while maintaining a supportive, trusting, and collaborative relationship.

Having spent 10 years living in India, Dr. Singh also brings a cross-cultural perspective to his work and strives to provide an inclusive environment for individuals and families from diverse backgrounds. He is proficient in English, Hindi, and Punjabi.
